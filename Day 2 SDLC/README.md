# Day 2: Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)

Welcome to Day 2. Today we focus on the industry standard process used to design, develop, and test software. Understanding SDLC is critical for every engineer—whether you are a developer, tester, or DevOps engineer—to ensure team alignment and product quality.

## 📅 Day 2 Curriculum

1. **[SDLC Overview](./sdlc-overview.md)**  
   *What is SDLC? The 6 phases of the lifecycle and why standards matter.*

2. **[DevOps in SDLC](./devops-in-sdlc.md)**  
   *Where does DevOps fit? Identifying the "DevOps-Centric" phases and how automation improves efficiency.*

3. **[Interview Prep: SDLC Questions](./interview-prep-sdlc.md)**  
   *How to answer "What is SDLC?" and "Which phases do you focus on?" in interviews.*

### 🎯 Learning Objectives for Day 2
- [ ] Define SDLC and its primary goal (delivering high-quality products).
- [ ] List the 6 phases: Planning, Defining, Designing, Building, Testing, Deployment.
- [ ] Identify which phases are primarily owned by DevOps Engineers.
- [ ] Understand the difference between HLD (High-Level Design) and LLD (Low-Level Design).

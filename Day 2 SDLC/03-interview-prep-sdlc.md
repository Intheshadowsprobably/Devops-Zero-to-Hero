# Interview Prep: SDLC Questions

## Q1: "What is SDLC?"
**Recommended Answer:**
> "SDLC stands for Software Development Life Cycle. It is a standard framework used by the software industry to design, develop, and test high-quality software. It typically consists of six phases: Planning, Defining Requirements, Designing, Building, Testing, and Deployment. Following SDLC ensures that we meet customer expectations and maintain code quality."

## Q2: "Which phases of SDLC are most important for a DevOps Engineer?"
**Recommended Answer:**
> "While I understand all phases, my primary focus as a DevOps engineer is on the **Building**, **Testing**, and **Deployment** phases. My goal is to automate these stages to reduce manual intervention, speed up delivery, and improve overall organizational efficiency."

## Q3: "What is the difference between HLD and LLD?"
**Recommended Answer:**
> "**High-Level Design (HLD)** focuses on the overall system architecture, such as database selection, scalability strategies, and high availability.
> **Low-Level Design (LLD)** goes into the granular details, such as specific function logic, module interactions, and API arguments. As a DevOps engineer, I usually interact more with HLD to understand infrastructure requirements like load balancing and database sizing."

## Q4: "Why is Planning considered a critical phase?"
**Recommended Answer:**
> "Planning allows us to gather requirements and validate ideas before writing any code. For example, if research shows customers don't want a 'Kids Catalog' feature, we can stop the project at the Planning stage, saving significant development and testing time."

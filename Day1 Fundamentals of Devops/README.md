# Day 1: Fundamentals of DevOps

Welcome to Day 1 of the DevOps Zero to Hero series. This session covers the foundational concepts, history, and cultural aspects of DevOps.

## Key Topics Covered
1. **What is DevOps?** – Understanding DevOps as a culture and process.
2. **Why DevOps?** – The evolution from traditional IT roles to modern DevOps practices.
3. **The 4 Pillars of DevOps** – Automation, Quality, Monitoring, and Testing.
4. **Interview Preparation** – How to introduce yourself and answer common Day 1 interview questions.

## Learning Objectives
- Define DevOps in your own words.
- Explain why DevOps was necessary (the shift from manual/siloed teams).
- Identify the four core pillars that ensure efficient delivery.
- Prepare a professional introduction for a DevOps engineering interview.
